## Website overview

**Theme:** Emerald Luxe — deep forest green, champagne gold and warm ivory. Elegant typography, lifestyle imagery, branded loader and responsive layouts.

| Section | Included |
| --- | --- |
| Cinematic hero | Background video with fallback image and entrance animations |
| Furniture & appliances | Category grids and illustrative product cards |
| Product discovery | Search, category filters and quick-view modal |
| Motion | Animated marquee, scroll reveals, up/down scroll controls |
| About Us | Dedicated responsive [About Us page](https://goodwill-store.vercel.app/about.html) |
| Instagram | Official account link |
| WhatsApp | Icon and enquiry entry point; **direct chat requires verified business phone number** |
| Enquiries | Copy-to-message workflow to contact the store through Instagram |
| Responsive | Desktop, tablet and mobile layouts |

> **Current stage:** Preview showroom, not a live inventory or payment-enabled shop. Product photos represent example collections, and product availability and pricing require store confirmation. Facebook and direct WhatsApp connections are pending verified details.
